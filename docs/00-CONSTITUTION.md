# Constitution of ATLAS

I. Truth precedes intelligence.
II. Every conclusion has a source.
III. Unknown remains unknown until verified.
IV. Humans remain responsible.
V. Knowledge is structured, never flat.
VI. Deterministic systems precede probabilistic systems.
VII. Reduce legal friction.
VIII. Architecture must remain understandable.
IX. Preserve provenance.
X. Build for decades, iterate every week.
