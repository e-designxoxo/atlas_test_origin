Machine Learning Roadmap

Learning layer, later

ATLAS becomes machine-learning aware only after the source model is reliable. The future learning layer will learn from structured documents, user notes, concept links, and verified citations — not from unsourced generation.

Structured memory Articles, paragraphs, annotations, bookmarks, concepts, source anchors.
Source traceability & notes Stable IDs, citation discipline, user notes grounded in document anchors.
Concept linking Connect legal concepts across articles, documents, and jurisdictions.
Retrieval intelligence Semantic search and embeddings to find relevant source units without inventing facts.
Personal study model Adapt revision prompts to what the student reads, saves, misunderstands, and revisits.
● No legal reasoning without source traceability. ATLAS is a knowledge operating system, not an AI lawyer.
